Compare

Swing is the candidate number's share of valid votes in 2026 minus its share in the 2022 first round, in percentage points. #13 compares Lula with Lula. #22 compares Flávio Bolsonaro (2026) with Jair Bolsonaro (2022), both PL.

A municipality is colored once at least 90% of its sections are counted, because early partial counts are skewed. The national figure uses only those municipalities, compared with the same places in 2022. Biggest moves list only places with at least 10,000 voters. Boa Esperança do Norte (MT) did not exist in 2022 and is left out.
